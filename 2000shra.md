### 2.1 Subsystem 1: Room Database Persistence & Entities

#### 1. Entity Architecture & Model Isolation
- Implemented [`FavoriteEntity`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/database/entity/FavoriteEntity.kt) and [`WatchlistEntity`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/database/entity/WatchlistEntity.kt) mapping Room storage to domain [`Movie`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/model/Movie.kt) objects via `toDomain()` and `fromDomain()`.
- Guarantees strict **collection isolation**: adding or removing a title from Favorites never affects the Watchlist (and vice-versa).
- Storage includes `movie_id` (PK), `title`, `poster_path`, `backdrop_path`, `release_date`, `vote_average`, `overview`, and `saved_timestamp`.

#### 2. Reactive DAOs & Repository Implementations
- [`FavoriteDao`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/database/dao/FavoriteDao.kt) & [`WatchlistDao`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/database/dao/WatchlistDao.kt):
  - Exposes reactive `Flow<List<FavoriteEntity>>` and `Flow<List<WatchlistEntity>>` ordered by `saved_timestamp DESC`.
  - Exposes reactive membership streams `isFavorite(movieId): Flow<Boolean>` and `isMovieInWatchlist(movieId): Flow<Boolean>`.
  - Exposes one-shot suspend queries (`isFavoriteOnce`, `isMovieInWatchlistOnce`, `getFavoriteById`, `getWatchlistMovieById`).
  - Supports atomic inserts (`OnConflictStrategy.REPLACE`) and removals (`deleteFavoriteById`, `deleteMovieById`).
- [`FavoritesRepositoryImpl`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/data/repository/impl/FavoritesRepositoryImpl.kt) & [`WatchlistRepositoryImpl`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/data/repository/impl/WatchlistRepositoryImpl.kt):
  - Bridges Room DAOs to the clean domain layer via coroutine Flow mapping.
  - Registered as singleton bindings in [`DatabaseModule`](file:///c:/Users/Ritunjay%20Deo/OneDrive/Desktop/MADVerse/app/src/main/java/com/example/madverse/core/database/DatabaseModule.kt).
