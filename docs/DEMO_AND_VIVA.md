# Demo and viva guide — 2026-10-21

Explain the flow: TMDB DTO → repository/domain → StateFlow ViewModel → Compose. Room supports the local library/preferences. Recommendations use explicit weighted factors rather than an external AI service. Promotional videos and rights-reviewed complete films use separate eligibility paths. YouTube owns delivery and playback controls; the app cannot guarantee or bypass availability.

Demo route: Discover → Search → Details → add Favorite and Watchlist → Library → restart and verify persistence → Quiz → Recommendations/reasons → Genres → Compare two films → Details regional providers (India, then another region) → Details trailer → Free Movies feature. Perform the playback portions only after their device acceptance checks pass. Explain the architecture after showing these user flows.

The score is Genre(30) + Mood(20) + Era(15) + Quality(15) + Language(10) + Runtime(10), clamped to 0–100. Missing optional metadata uses neutral handling in the individual scoring functions. Candidates are deduplicated by movie ID; ties sort by audience rating, vote count, popularity and finally ascending ID. Match reasons come from the factors actually scored, not generated prose or a critic-rating service. The percentage is a weighted match score, not a probability.

Exact current scoring: genre overlap divided by selected-genre count times 30, rounded (15 if no selection); mood-genre overlap divided by the mood's genre count times 20, rounded (10 for ANY). Era earns 15 for ANY/missing year or a matching range (classic through 1989, modern 1990–2014, contemporary from 2015), otherwise 0. Audience quality earns 0 below the minimum rating, otherwise round(min(rating, 10) / 10 × 15); popularity affects reasons and tie-breaking, not extra score points. Language earns 10 for no preference/missing metadata or a case-insensitive match, otherwise 0. Runtime earns 10 for ANY/missing/nonpositive duration or a matching range (quick below 100, standard 100–140, epic above 140), otherwise 0. The six integer factors are summed and clamped to 0–100.
